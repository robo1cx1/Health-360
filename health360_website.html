Project overview

About Health360

A data visualization project designed to make patterns in a patient dataset easier to explore and communicate.

Project objective

Health360 explores how patient demographics and lifestyle indicators vary alongside health outcomes in a 5,000-record dataset. The dashboard supports interactive exploration, while the story presents the analysis in a clear sequence.

Tableau PublicData VisualizationHealthcare AnalyticsRisk FactorsLifestyle Analysis

What you'll explore

  • Patient distribution across age groups
  • BMI categories and heart disease prevalence
  • Diabetes and heart disease comparisons
  • Exercise, smoking and alcohol patterns
  • Blood pressure and fatigue across age groups
Interpretation note: These visualizations describe patterns and associations in the supplied dataset. They do not establish that a lifestyle factor or demographic characteristic causes a health outcome, and they are not intended for clinical diagnosis or medical decision-making.
